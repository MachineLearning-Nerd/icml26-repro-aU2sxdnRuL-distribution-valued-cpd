# Limitations and deviations

- This is concrete numerical evidence from the paper's original figure, not an independent experiment on raw FCS files.
- The paper does not define “approximately.” The contract grants a generous 50% tolerance, accepting values through 1.5 as approximately 1.
- The public FlowRepository manifest is accessible, but all tested official FCS download endpoints redirect unauthenticated requests to login.
- This route does not validate the authors' sampling seeds, feature preprocessing, OT implementation, or classification protocol.
